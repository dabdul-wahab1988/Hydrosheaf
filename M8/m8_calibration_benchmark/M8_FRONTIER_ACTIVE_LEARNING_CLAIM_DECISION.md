# M8 frontier active-learning claim decision

Run: `RUN-M8-FRONTIER-AL-20260728-01`

Status: **PASS for a bounded controlled-synthetic scientific workflow**

External or field status: **not earned**

## Decision

The new `bayesian_active_learning` method has earned scientific-workflow status
for controlled synthetic topology-measurement design. It also meets the
predeclared definition of bounded active-learning excellence: the method passed
its production contract and invariance tests, the untouched benchmark passed
every frozen scientific gate, and all seven registered outputs regenerated
byte-identically.

“Professorial” and “frontier” are not standard statistical certifications. In
this project they refer to methodological rigor: explicit hypotheses and
likelihoods, decision utility, resource costs, finite-scenario model-discrepancy
analysis, posterior updating, batch redundancy control, abstention, independent
truth generation, prospective locking, strong comparators, paired uncertainty
intervals, and immutable provenance. They do not mean universal superiority.

## What passed

- Mean candidate recall was 0.9861 and actionability was 1.00 across 24
  independent MODFLOW 6/MODPATH 7 aquifer cases.
- Versus random acquisition, median Brier difference was -0.02483 with a 95%
  paired bootstrap interval of [-0.02897, -0.01919].
- Versus random acquisition, median joint-entropy reduction per cost improved
  by 0.03581 nats per relative-cost unit [0.02206, 0.04218].
- Versus the strong legacy uncertainty-chemistry policy, Brier difference was
  +0.00148 [0.00000, 0.00582], within the frozen +0.01 noninferiority margin.
- Versus legacy, information-efficiency difference was -0.00422
  [-0.00955, 0.00000], just within the frozen -0.01 noninferiority margin.
- PR-AUC was not materially harmed, all 25 focused tests passed, static checks
  passed, and the authoritative and reproduction outputs were byte-identical.

## What did not pass or was not demonstrated

- Superiority over the strong legacy policy was **not** established. The new
  method was slightly worse in the median on both locked legacy comparisons,
  although within the frozen noninferiority margins.
- The information-efficiency noninferiority result is close to its margin: its
  lower interval is only 0.00045 nats per cost above the failure boundary.
- The robust and mean policies chose different action sequences in 15 of 24
  cases, but no performance superiority of robustness over the mean policy was
  established.
- All 120 proposed-policy actions were chemistry panels. Age and direct
  connectivity actions were available to the algorithm but were not selected
  under the declared costs and likelihoods. Multimodal usefulness therefore
  remains a capability, not an empirical contribution from this run.
- Batch redundancy control passed focused tests but was not evaluated as an
  end-to-end batch field-design strategy because the locked benchmark was
  sequential.
- There is no prospective real-aquifer, operator, temporal-series,
  three-dimensional or vadose-zone validation.

## Allowed claim

> Across 24 untouched, independently generated controlled-synthetic aquifer
> cases, scenario-robust Bayesian acquisition improved topology Brier score and
> joint-hypothesis information per declared cost over random acquisition and
> was noninferior, within prospectively frozen margins, to a strong
> uncertainty-priority chemistry policy. The workflow and seven registered
> outputs regenerated byte-identically.

Do not shorten this to “HydroSheaf active learning is superior,” “field
validated,” or “optimal.”
