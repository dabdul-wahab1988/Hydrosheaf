# Thesis objective–evidence alignment

## Data-limited Ghana objective

Objective 6 is:

> Apply the framework and its component diagnostics to Ghanaian aquifer
> datasets to determine which integrated interpretations are supportable under
> available data and which remain non-identifiable.

The matching research question is:

> Which components and integrated interpretations of the proposed framework
> are supportable from available Ghanaian aquifer data, and which remain
> non-identifiable?

This wording is deliberate. It does not imply that the Northern Ghana dataset
contains environmental age tracers, exact coordinates, screen intervals,
time-varying hydraulic heads, or independently confirmed flow paths.

## Available Ghana evidence

The Northern Ghana workbook contains:

- 160 wells and 320 wet/dry hydrochemical records;
- major ions, fluoride, stable water isotopes, Sr and dissolved SiO2;
- calcite, dolomite, gypsum and halite saturation indices;
- total borehole depth, elevation and one static-water-level field;
- regional geology, lithology, aquifer and land-use classes; and
- processed graph edges and masked coordinates.

It does not contain:

- an environmental age-tracer panel suitable for residence-time inversion;
- screen-top and screen-bottom intervals;
- exact public borehole coordinates;
- a repeated hydraulic-head time series;
- field-confirmed directed connectivity; or
- field reaction-mechanism truth.

Elevation minus static water level can be used only as a single-occasion head
proxy. Because coordinates are displaced by approximately 1–3 km and the
released graph is a processed analytical output, neither may be represented as
independent site-scale connectivity truth.

## Permitted Ghana claims

The field evidence can support:

1. data-readiness and minimum-measurement audits;
2. seasonal hydrochemistry hold-forward prediction;
3. reaction-family plausibility and equivalence-class reporting;
4. sensitivity to alternative assumed edge sets;
5. null explanations for apparent connectivity; and
6. identification of interpretations that remain non-identifiable.

It cannot support field validation of:

1. groundwater residence time;
2. exact directed flow paths;
3. screen-resolved vertical connectivity;
4. unique reaction mechanisms; or
5. a fully observed age–topology–reaction digital twin.

## Objective-aligned next benchmark

M7.3 evaluates integration as conditional uncertainty reduction rather than
assuming that adding evidence must improve accuracy. It compares isolated,
pairwise and fully integrated evidence under native, weakened and deliberately
misspecified conditions. A reduction in uncertainty counts as beneficial only
when calibration or predictive skill is not materially degraded.

The required outputs are:

- topology-to-age sensitivity under correct, partial and reversed connectivity
  assumptions;
- edgewise uncertainty and predictive skill for hydraulics, age, chemistry and
  their combinations;
- conflict detection when one evidence stream is misspecified;
- reaction-family support entropy under core and enhanced chemistry; and
- a machine-readable Ghana data-scope audit enforcing the claim boundary above.
