**Supplementary Table S6 | External pathline-time and endpoint-projection diagnostics for the Savage MODPATH archive.**
Pathline-time diagnostics were calculated to evaluate how MODPATH endpoint and pathline outputs were compressed into graph-compatible evidence. Endpoint projection was fully preserved at the particle and edge levels, and deterministic edge-agreement metrics were complete within the processed archive. **Supplementary Table S9 | Node-sparsity sensitivity of head-gradient Hydrosheaf topology inference.**
Node-sparsity sensitivity was evaluated by randomly subsampling the benchmark node set across fractions from 10% to 100% and recalculating topology agreement between the head-gradient Hydrosheaf graph and the MODPATH-derived reference graph. Zero-imputation was applied: trials in which fewer than five reference edges survived in the node-induced subgraph were included in the mean as F1 = 0 (precision = 0, recall = 0) rather than being discarded. This corrects the survivorship bias present in earlier results, where discarding failed trials artificially inflated mean F1 at low node fractions. After zero-imputation, performance collapses sharply below 50% node density (mean F1 = 0.149 at 10%, 0.251 at 25%) before recovering toward the full-node benchmark (F1 = 0.618 at 100%). Results should be interpreted as a controlled robustness diagnostic, not as a field-scale sampling uncertainty model.
