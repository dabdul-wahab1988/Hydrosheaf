# Academic Manuscript Structure & Formatting Rules

When drafting, assembling, revising, or compiling academic manuscripts (e.g., for *Computers & Geosciences*, Elsevier, AGU, Copernicus, or Springer):

## 1. Canonical Section Ordering (IMRAD Structure)
The main text must flow uninterrupted through the narrative sections:
1. **Title, Authors, and Affiliations**
2. **Abstract, Keywords, Highlights**
3. **1. Introduction**
4. **2. Computational Framework / Methods / Theory**
5. **3. Benchmark and Validation Design**
6. **4. Results**
7. **5. Discussion** (*MUST follow Section 4 directly without intervening tables or figures*)
8. **6. Conclusions**
9. **Back Matter:**
   - Code and Data Availability
   - Funding
   - Competing Interests
   - CRediT Authorship Statement
   - AI Declaration
   - Acknowledgements
10. **References**
11. **Tables** (*All tables with captions, notes, and full cell contents appear AFTER References*)
12. **Figure Captions** (*Complete numbered figure captions list appears AFTER Tables*)
13. **Figures** (*Standalone high-resolution image files or embedded figures following captions*)

## 2. Prohibited Layout Patterns
- **NEVER insert tables or figure captions between Results and Discussion.**
- **NEVER insert tables or figure captions before Conclusions or References.**
- In-text display item references must use citations/callouts (e.g., Table 1, Figure 2), while the actual tables and figure caption blocks reside strictly at the end of the manuscript after References.
