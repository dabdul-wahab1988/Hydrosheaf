7. Conclusions and Recommendations

Key Findings

  • Evaporative Concentration: All flow paths show gamma > 1.0, indicating evaporation is a significant process in water chemistry evolution.
  • Active Denitrification: Substantial denitrification occurs along vadose-to-groundwater pathways, particularly during wet season when nitrate inputs are highest.
  • Mineral Dissolution: Halite, gypsum, and calcite dissolution consistently increase TDS along flow paths.
  • Ion Exchange: Ca-Na and Mg-Na exchange reactions modify cation ratios, indicating clay mineral interactions.
  • Seasonal Dynamics: Wet season shows highest NO3 concentrations linked to fertilizer leaching; dry season shows natural attenuation.

Recommendations

  1. Implement fertilizer timing management to reduce wet season leaching peaks
  2. Monitor denitrification capacity in the vadose zone as a natural attenuation buffer
  3. Consider additional age tracers (CFCs, SF6) to constrain residence time estimates
  4. Evaluate reactive barriers if nitrate attenuation needs enhancement
