This folder contains synthetic (trial) CSVs for Hydrosheaf testing.

NEW metadata added in this version:
- fertilizer_applications.csv: fertiliser product/type, application dates, estimated N rates per cluster (Cluster A/B in proposal).
- irrigation_by_event.csv: irrigation amount in previous 7 days per cluster (to complement rain_7d_mm in events.csv).
- land_management.csv: land-use and management descriptors per cluster.
- vadose_zone_metadata.csv: soil-profile rows enriched with texture, SOC, pH, Ksat, water-table depth estimate, and denitrification potential index.
- redox_proxies.csv: DO/ORP/DOC/Fe2/Mn2/NH4/NO2/N2O as attenuation-relevant proxies by station and event.
- geology_mineralogy.csv: study-area-informed lookup of likely lithology/mineral phases (still synthetic at station scale).

All original CSVs are preserved.
